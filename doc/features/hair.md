# Hair — Chiang + Huang BCSDFs, strand vertex AOVs

Status: implemented (host + device). `hairmat` material with two scattering
models — `model=chiang` (default) and `model=huang` — plus per-vertex strand
AOVs that feed Cycles "Hair Info" outputs.

## What and why

Two pieces make production hair shading work:

1. **A hair BSDF.** A fibre BCSDF with longitudinal roughness and a
   Melanin/eumelanin-pheomelanin absorption model gives the characteristic
   glints + colour of real hair — far better than a diffuse/glossy
   approximation.
2. **Per-strand varying attributes.** Cycles' Hair Info node needs `strand u`
   (parametric coordinate along the fibre) and a per-strand random value.
   These are stored as **vertex AOVs** on the tessellated strand mesh and
   readable in shading.

## References

- Chiang, Bitterli, Tappan, Burley. **A Practical and Controllable Hair and Fur
  Model for Production Path Tracing.** Computer Graphics Forum 35(2), 2016.
  (The `chiang` model — longitudinal/azimuthal roughness + melanin.)
- Huang, Weidlich, Hullin, Hanika. **A Microfacet-based Hair Scattering
  Model.** EGSR 2022. (The `huang` model — Blender's Principled Hair v2;
  reference implementation: Cycles `bsdf_principled_hair_huang.h`,
  Apache-2.0.)

## Models

### `model=chiang` (default)

Far-field Marschner lobes: R / TT / TRT / TRRT+ with `beta_m` (longitudinal)
and `beta_n` (azimuthal) roughness, `eta`, `alpha` (cuticle tilt). Backward
compatible — scenes without `model` render identically.

The far-field model integrates over the strand cross-section and assumes a
fixed azimuthal mapping; on `solid` tessellation (real cylindrical geometry)
grazing hits lose ~40% energy in a furnace test. On `ribbon` tessellation
(the intended domain) it conserves ~90%.

### `model=huang`

Near-field microfacet model: the strand surface is a GGX rough dielectric
cylinder/ellipsoid evaluated at the actual hit point. Energy conservation is
much better on real geometry (furnace σa=0 → 0.97 vs Chiang's 0.46 on the
same solid tessellation).

Parameters:

- `roughness` — GGX microfacet roughness (α), replaces beta_m/beta_n.
- `aspectratio` — cross-section minor/major axis ratio b ∈ (0,1]; 1 = circle.
  Elliptical strands have a narrower projected silhouette, so an `aspectratio`
  furnace legitimately reads < 1 (same behaviour as Cycles).
- `scale_r`, `scale_tt`, `scale_trt` — per-lobe energy scales (artistic,
  non-physical when < 1).
- Shared with Chiang: `eta`, `alpha` (tilt), and all absorption modes
  (`sigma_a`, `color`, `eumelanin`/`pheomelanin`).

Implementation notes:

- The azimuthal hit offset h comes from the real surface geometry (shading
  normal × tangent) — no cross-section integration is needed at a fixed hit,
  so eval reduces to the surface form `f·cos = K/(arc·cosMi)` of the paper's
  curve kernel (R lobe = slanted-GGX BRDF `F·D·G·scale/(4·cosMi)`, verified
  against brute-force microfacet MC).
- `Evaluate` uses deterministic VNDF quadrature (Hammersley points) for the
  residual TT/TRT lobes since it must be a pure function of (wi, wo);
  `Sample` uses Cycles' energy-proportional self-normalized estimator
  (f/pdf = eval, pdfW = 1).
- A GGX-dielectric directional-albedo LUT (`hair_huang_lut.h` /
  `hair_huang_lut.cl`, generated by `dev-tools/e36_gen_huang_lut.py`)
  supplies the single-scattering energy compensation 1/E — same role as
  Cycles' `ggx_glass_E` tables.
- **Sign convention trap**: `FresnelDielectricT` returns a *positive*
  transmitted cosine while Cycles' `fresnel_dielectric` returns a negative
  one. `RefractAngle` call sites negate it, otherwise wt points above the
  surface and all TT/TRT visibility guards reject the path.

## Implementation

- `hairmat` material — both models share absorption parameterization
  (melanin/direct colour/σa), `eta`, `alpha`. Host (CPU) and device
  (OpenCL/Metal) paths mirror each other (`materialdefs_funcs_hair.cl`).
- `StrandsShape` writes `strand-u`, `strand-random`, and the tangent AOVs
  Huang needs for the true geometric azimuth; Cycles Hair Info → these AOVs
  via the node reader.
- `cyHairFile` out-of-bounds read fixed.
- Hair geometry: native Metal Catmull-Rom curve primitives landed
  (`4a90e7057573da31442615f36fa213c53ec3d9bd`, E7) on the Metal HWRT path; tessellation remains the
  fallback. Emissive strands force the tessellated path (`fe11c2ee900d1c19119e488f8f6ea0f865b5cc39`)
  so sampling geometry matches intersection geometry.
- SuperBlendLuxCore: native Hair material node (`model`, `roughness`,
  `aspectratio`, lobe scales, all colorization modes) and Cycles Principled
  Hair reader mapping (model enum CHIANG/HUANG → `model`).
